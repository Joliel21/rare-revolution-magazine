# Series styling rules

These rules are approved for the current RARE Revolution Magazine build and should be preserved during future layout work.

## Page-type abbreviations

Use these terms consistently when discussing or editing the magazine:

- **SCS — Series Cover Spread:** the main two-page cover/divider spread for a major series.
- **SSTP — Sub-Series Title Page:** the title/introduction page for a sub-series within a major series.
- **AP — Article Page:** the facing/archive/gallery/content page that contains article cards, article listings, or other article content for the sub-series.

The Charity & Advocacy page showing the article-card grid is an example of an **AP**.

## Major series cover spreads (SCS)

Major series cover/divider spreads must remain visually intact as designed. **Do not add a generated series-color footer bar to any major series cover spread.**

This includes, at minimum:

- RARE INSIGHTS main cover spread
- People of Rare main cover spread
- Digital Spotlight main cover spread
- RARE Reports main cover spread
- Charity Partners main cover spread
- Resources main cover spread

If a future major series cover spread is added, it should follow the same rule unless explicitly approved otherwise.

## Sub-series title pages (SSTP)

A sub-series title page is **not** a major series cover spread.

**Do not add a generated bottom colored bar to any SSTP.**

This rule applies to every sub-series title page across all major series, including RARE INSIGHTS SSTPs such as **A Day in the Life**.

## Article pages (AP)

Article pages are the article/archive/gallery/content pages associated with a sub-series. They keep the applicable series-color styling, including the approved footer treatment for that series.

**The background directly behind the article cards/listings on an AP must use that major series' color. Do not recolor the entire page.**

The AP header/title area remains as designed, and the article cards themselves remain as designed. Only the article browsing/content field behind the cards uses the series color.

Examples:

- RARE INSIGHTS AP article field: gold
- People of Rare AP article field: black
- Digital Spotlight AP article field: bright blue
- Charity Partners AP article field: bright green
- RARE Reports AP article field: purple
- Resources AP article field: teal

**All AP scrollbars are black, regardless of series color.** This includes the scrollbar thumb and the scrollbar arrow buttons. The scrollbar track may remain neutral so the black controls stay visible.

APs keep the applicable series-color bottom bar unless explicitly changed.

This distinction is permanent unless explicitly changed:

- **SCS / Series Cover Spread:** no generated footer bar.
- **SSTP / Sub-Series Title Page:** no generated footer bar.
- **AP / Article Page:** keep the applicable series-color footer treatment, use the series color behind the articles only, and use black scrollbar controls.

## Interior and sub-series pages

Generated series footer styling belongs on approved APs, not on SCS or SSTP pages.

Do not infer that the AP footer rule applies to a series cover spread or a sub-series title page.
